About CICI 2027

The International Congress of Engineering Sciences (CICI 2027) will bring together researchers, academics, professionals, and students to present and discuss recent developments, applications, and emerging technologies across engineering disciplines.

The congress will promote scientific exchange and collaboration in areas related to intelligent engineering systems and industrial digital transformation.

Dates
July 13–14, 2027
Location
Latacunga, Ecuador
Organizer
Universidad Técnica de Cotopaxi (UTC), Ecuador
Expected attendance
Approximately 200 participants

Conference Topics

  • Artificial Intelligence and Optimization in Engineering Systems
  • Smart Manufacturing and Industry 4.0
  • Internet of Things and Digital Twins
  • Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Industrial Automation and Intelligent Control
  • Energy Systems, Renewable Energy and Smart Grids
  • Extended Reality and Immersive Technologies in Engineering
  • Data-Driven Engineering and Industrial Digital Transformation
  • Robotics and Intelligent Systems
  • Engineering Education and Emerging Technologies
